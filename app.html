Practice Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out how long metronome practice takes from the numbers you type. The tempos, steps, measures and time budget are yours to choose. It only counts beats, so it has not heard you play and does not know your instrument, your level, your piece or whether a tempo or a ramp is right for you, and it gives no verdict on your playing or your progress. Every number is your own entry.

Practice Math

One stop of 8 measures at 60 BPM takes 32 seconds. The full ramp from 60 to 120 BPM in steps of 5 takes 4 minutes 50 seconds across 13 stops.

Boundary: arithmetic only Practice Math works out how long metronome practice takes from the numbers you type. The tempos, steps, measures and time budget are yours to choose. It only counts beats, so it has not heard you play and does not know your instrument, your level, your piece or whether a tempo or a ramp is right for you, and it gives no verdict on your playing or your progress. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: 8 measures of 4 beats per stop, a ramp from 60 to 120 BPM in steps of 5, and a 10 minute practice block.

The stop truth

One stop of 8 measures at 4 beats each makes 32 beats, which at 60 BPM takes 32 seconds.

The ramp truth

From 60 to 120 BPM in steps of 5 the ramp holds 13 stops. At 8 measures of 4 beats per stop the whole ramp takes 4 minutes 50 seconds.

The budget truth

With 10 minutes on the clock, you finish 13 stops, ending at 120 BPM, in 4 minutes 50 seconds. That is the whole ramp, with 5 minutes 10 seconds to spare.
